# Politics in Name Only

### Do America's politicians vote the way they say they will?

An open, evidence-first research project examining the gap between what elected officials claim to stand for and what their recorded actions show, across both major parties, using the same method for each.

---

## Status

**Early development.** No data or findings are published yet.

## What this is

A long-form investigation into party labels, stated promises, and recorded votes in US politics, built so that anyone can check the work.

## What to expect

- **Definitions first.** Every term and measure will be published, cited,   and version-locked *before* any results are released.
- **Symmetric by design.** One methodology, applied identically to both parties.
- **Open evidence.** Every claim will link back to a primary public record.
- **Plain language.** Written to be understood without a political science degree.
- **Published limitations.** What can't be measured will be stated openly.

## Planned outputs

- A research paper
- An open methodology and source register
- A public website (companion repository, coming later)
